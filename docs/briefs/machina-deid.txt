Build a pipeline that replaces identifying information with consistent pseudonyms while keeping every engineering file usable and the links between them intact.You do not need an AWS account. Download the input from https://machina-input.joinbuilders.org, run your pipeline, upload your output to your team's Builders folder with upload.py, and submit that location on this page.1. Get the inputDownload the corpus from https://machina-input.joinbuilders.org into ./input/. Use a case-sensitive filesystem with room for the 139.7 GB input, unpacked archives, and your output.Download the script: curl -fsSLO https://machina-input.joinbuilders.org/get-input.shTest on 100 files first: bash get-input.sh --limit 100Download the full corpus: bash get-input.shYou can re-run get-input.sh anytime to skip completed files, resume incomplete downloads, and verify file sizes. Run bash get-input.sh --help for --prefix and --jobs options, or fetch https://machina-input.joinbuilders.org/manifest.tsv for every file path and size.On a slow connection, skip the download to your own machine: rent a cloud VM on your own account with any provider, with at least 400 GB of disk, and run get-input.sh, your pipeline, and upload.py there.Keep the raw input private. Do not publish or share it.2. Build and run your pipelineProcess the full corpus with a reproducible pipeline on any machine. Keep temporary files and private mappings out of your submission folder.Replace identifying company and person names, accounts, contact details, domains, addresses, logos, and identifying project or part names and numbers. Give the same identity one consistent pseudonym across the corpus, keep distinct identities separate, and fill identity fields with meaningful pseudonyms rather than blanking them.Cover visible text and images, file and folder names, PDF metadata and attachments, native CAD properties, embedded previews, and archive contents. Inspect internal metadata in native files: a plain text search or a clean preview is not enough.Preserve geometry, dimensions, tolerances, units, materials, configurations, BOMs, and links between drawings, models, and assemblies. Update references when you rename a file, keep usable native files (not only exported PDFs or images), and leave format-required keys and non-identifying engineering data unchanged.The input has duplicates and some invalid or empty files. Do not silently drop difficult engineering files. Explain source defects and omissions in README.txt using final paths or neutral descriptions, and leave no original identifiers inside output archives.3. Prepare your attempt folderGive each attempt its own attempt-N/ folder containing a full copy of your output and code:attempt-N/corpus/ holds the de-identified files and archives.attempt-N/code/ holds the complete pipeline source and dependency versions or lockfiles. Identifier-matching logic is allowed here; scoring reads corpus/.attempt-N/README.txt gives exact run instructions, limitations, and exceptions.Exclude credentials, raw input, temporary files, and private run logs.4. Upload and submitDownload upload.py below (Python 3, nothing to install), then press Get upload token in the submission panel. The token lasts 1 hour and writes only to your team's private Builders folder, gs://builders-machina-submissions-1120/teams/<your-team-id>/.Run the upload.py command shown in the panel to upload corpus/, code/, and README.txt into attempt-N/. If the token expires mid-upload, get a new one and paste it when upload.py prompts; re-running upload.py skips files already uploaded.Submit gs://builders-machina-submissions-1120/teams/<your-team-id>/attempt-N/ in the form on this page. You do not need to look up your team id: after Get upload token, the panel shows your folder with it filled in, and Use in submit box copies it into the form. Files in your Builders folder are deleted 10 days after upload. Leave submitted attempts unchanged until results are published.How it is scoredEach attempt is scored out of 1,000 private checks on corpus/, with pass counts by category (never which checks or files). A check on a file the scorer cannot find or open fails (and shows as not checked), so a broken or missing file costs points. Keep each file's extension and the parts of its path that do not identify anyone so the scorer can find the files it checks.Grading usually takes under 20 minutes once started, and at most 3 hours. Your team has 20 scored attempts in total and may submit twice an hour.Work at MachinaMachina is hiring Engineering Researchers.
